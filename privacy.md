# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026  
Version: 1.0

AA Prime Studio ("we") provides Folio: Photo Albums (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what data the App processes, where it is processed, how long it is kept, and how you can exercise your rights.  

## 1. Summary

- Your albums and photos stay on your device. No account is required, and we do not run servers for the App.
- Photos in an album's private layer are encrypted on your device. We never have your passcode, your recovery key, or your encryption keys, so we cannot see, unlock, or recover your private photos.
- We do not sell personal data, show ads, or use analytics, advertising, or cross-app tracking tools.
- Purchases are handled by Apple. We do not receive your name or payment details.

## 2. Data Processed on Your Device

### 2.1 Albums and Everyday Photos

Each album has an everyday layer that anyone using your device can see. Album names, covers, and everyday-layer photos are stored in the App's private storage on your device, protected by iOS data protection. They are not encrypted by the App, because they are meant to be visible.

### 2.2 Private Photos

Photos you move into an album's private layer, their thumbnails, and the list of which photos belong to which album are encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM before they are saved. The encryption key is stored in your device's Keychain and can be unlocked only with your App passcode, with Face ID if you turn it on, or with your recovery key. Encrypted files are stored under random names, so they do not reveal which album they belong to.

### 2.3 Passcode, Face ID, and Recovery Key

- Your App passcode is never stored. The App stores your encryption key locked with a key derived from your passcode.
- Face ID is handled entirely by iOS. The App never receives your face data; it only learns whether Face ID succeeded.
- Your recovery key is shown to you once when you set up the App. We do not store it and cannot show it to you again. If you lose both your passcode and your recovery key, your private photos cannot be recovered by you or by us.

### 2.4 Your Photos Library

- When you add photos, you choose them in Apple's photo picker. The App receives only the photos you select.
- If, after moving photos into a private layer, you choose to delete the originals from your Photos library, the App asks for access to your Photos library only to delete those specific photos, and iOS asks you to confirm. Deleted photos stay in Recently Deleted in the Photos app according to Apple's settings.
- If you save a photo back to Photos, the App asks only for permission to add photos.

### 2.5 Encrypted Backups

If you export a backup, the App creates one encrypted file containing your albums and photos, including album names and everyday-layer photos. You choose where to save it, for example iCloud Drive or another location in the Files app. That location's provider stores the file under its own terms and privacy policy, but cannot read its contents without your recovery key.

### 2.6 Device Backups

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your device to a computer, those backups may include the App's data. Private photos remain encrypted in those backups. The encryption key itself is not included, so after restoring to a new device you will need your recovery key.

### 2.7 Folio Plus (In-App Purchases)

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, payment card, Apple Account, or billing address. The App checks with Apple on your device whether you have an active purchase. Apple provides us with sales reports that do not identify you personally. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account settings.

## 3. Data We Do Not Collect

The App does not send us, and we do not collect:

- your photos, album names, or covers;
- your passcode, recovery key, or encryption keys;
- your name, email address, phone number, or contacts;
- your location;
- usage analytics, crash analytics, advertising identifiers, or tracking data.

## 4. Retention and Deletion

- Albums and photos: kept on your device until you delete them in the App or remove the App.
- Deleting an album does not delete the private photos it contained; you can recover them from the App's settings.
- Encrypted backup files: kept wherever you saved them until you delete them.
- Removing the App deletes its data from your device, except copies kept in device backups or backup files you exported.
- Support and data-rights requests: kept as long as needed to handle the request and meet legal requirements.

## 5. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not have your data, so we cannot share it. Data leaves your device only:

1. when you save a backup file or share or save a photo to a place you choose;
2. when Apple system services, such as iCloud Backup, operate according to your settings; or
3. when you email us.

If we receive a legal request, we can provide only what we actually hold, such as emails you have sent us.

## 6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

## 7. Your Rights and Choices

To the extent provided by applicable law, you may ask us to give you access to, correct, stop using, or delete personal data we hold, or withdraw consent, and you may lodge a complaint with the competent authority.

Because your albums and photos stay on your device, you control them directly:

- delete photos, albums, or the App at any time;
- turn Face ID or Photos access on or off in iOS Settings; and
- email us to make a data-rights request about anything you have sent us.

## 8. Security

The App protects your private photos with on-device encryption, a separate App passcode, attempt limits after wrong passcodes, automatic locking when you leave an album or the App, and a privacy screen in the App Switcher. No device, backup, or method is completely secure, and anyone who knows your App passcode or recovery key can open your private photos. If an incident occurs that may materially affect your rights, we will take the steps required by applicable law.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when features, data practices, laws, or platform requirements change. We will give reasonable notice of material changes in the App, in release notes, or by another reasonable method, and will ask for consent again where the law requires it.
